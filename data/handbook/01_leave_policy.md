# Leave Policy

## Annual vacation
Full-time employees at Meridian Labs receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year. After 3 completed years of service this increases to 27 days, and after 5 completed years of service to 30 days. Part-time employees receive vacation pro rata to their contracted hours. Vacation accrues monthly from the start date.

## Requesting vacation
Vacation is requested in PeopleHub. Requests covering more than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance. Managers respond within 3 working days. Only working days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays) count against the vacation balance.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire without compensation.

## Public holidays and year-end shutdown
Meridian Labs observes 10 public holidays per year. The offices are also closed from December 24 to December 31. These shutdown days do not count against the vacation balance.

## Sick leave
Employees receive up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required from the 4th consecutive sick day. Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave can be taken at any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.
