# Information Security Policy

## Accounts and passwords
All employees must store credentials in the company password manager, Vaultline. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account. SMS codes are not accepted; use an authenticator app or a hardware key.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB storage devices are blocked by default; exceptions need approval from the security team.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen device to security@meridianlabs.example within 1 hour of noticing the loss. The security team will remotely wipe the device.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment data and health records, may only be stored in approved systems and never on laptops or personal cloud storage.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the "Report Phish" button in the mail client. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.

## Public networks
Always use the company VPN when connecting from public Wi-Fi networks.

## Security training
New hires must complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining. All employees repeat the training every 12 months.
