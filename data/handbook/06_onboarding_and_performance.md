# Onboarding and Performance

## Before day one
IT ships the laptop so that it arrives at least 2 working days before the start date. Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy.

## Probation
The probation period lasts 6 months. The manager holds check-ins with the new hire at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Performance reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and in December. Ratings use a 1 to 5 scale, where 3 means "meets expectations". Promotions are decided once a year in the January cycle.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of €1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences. In addition, employees get up to 3 days of paid conference leave per year.

## Referral bonus
Employees who refer a successful hire receive a bonus of €2,000, paid after the new hire completes probation.
