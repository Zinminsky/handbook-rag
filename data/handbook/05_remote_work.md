# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Office attendance
Meridian Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office at least 2 days per week. Each team chooses its own anchor days.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET. Outside core hours employees may schedule their work flexibly.

## Focus time
Wednesday afternoons, after 12:00, are reserved for focus work. No internal meetings may be scheduled then.

## Working from abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with written approval from their manager and from HR. Longer stays are not permitted because of tax and social security rules.

## Coworking
Employees who live more than 30 km from the nearest office receive a coworking membership budget of €100 per month.

## Equipment
IT provides a laptop and one external monitor. Additional equipment is covered by the home-office budget described in the expenses policy.
