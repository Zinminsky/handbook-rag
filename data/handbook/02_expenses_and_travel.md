# Expenses and Travel Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date using the Spendly app. A receipt is required for every expense over €25. Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 working days of manager approval.

## Meals
During business travel the daily meal allowance is €45 for domestic trips and €70 for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances, including client dinners.

## Accommodation
Hotel stays are capped at €180 per night. In Zurich, London and New York the cap is raised to €250 per night. Bookings above the cap need written approval from the traveler's VP before booking.

## Flights and ground transport
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from the CFO. Rail travel is second class; first class is allowed for journeys longer than 4 hours. Use of a private car is reimbursed at €0.30 per km.

## Trip approvals
Any single trip expected to cost more than €2,000 must be approved in advance by a VP.

## Home office budget
Every employee has a one-time home-office equipment budget of €800 that renews every 3 years. Remote and hybrid employees also receive an internet stipend of €40 per month.
